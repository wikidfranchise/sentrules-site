FAQ

Before you connect your mailbox.

Why can't Outlook just do this itself?

Classic Outlook offered “Apply rule on messages I send.” New Outlook's rules apply to mail you receive. People have been asking Microsoft for the sent-mail equivalent repeatedly — the threads above are a sample. SentRules exists because the answer is still no.

Do I have to install an Outlook add-in?

No. SentRules runs as a hosted service. An Outlook ribbon shortcut can be added later as a convenience.

Does SentRules send my emails?

No. You send from Outlook normally. SentRules only works on the confirmed sent copy afterwards.

What Microsoft permissions does it request?

User.Read, Mail.Read and Mail.ReadWrite — enough to identify the account, inspect the sent copy and move it between folders. It does not request Mail.Send.

What if two rules disagree?

SentRules does not pick a winner. The sent copy stays in Sent Items until you resolve the conflict.

Can I cancel?

Yes — from Stripe billing inside SentRules Manager. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing.
