FAQ

Before you connect your mailbox.

Do I have to install an Outlook add-in?

No. SentRules launches as a hosted service. The Outlook ribbon version can be added later as a convenience shortcut when Microsoft Store distribution is available.

Does SentRules send my emails?

No. You send from Outlook normally. SentRules works on the confirmed sent copy afterward.

What Microsoft permissions does the production service request?

User.Read, Mail.Read, and Mail.ReadWrite. Those permissions let SentRules identify the connected account, inspect the sent copy, and move it between Outlook folders. The production background-filing service does not request Mail.Send.

What if two rules disagree?

SentRules does not choose a winner. The sent copy stays in Sent Items until you fix the conflicting rules.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Paying customers can open Stripe subscription management from SentRules Manager.
