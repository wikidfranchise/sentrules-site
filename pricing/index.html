PRICING FAQ

No pricing games.

The introductory offer is tied to real paid licenses, not a fake countdown clock.

What exactly does “Founding 500” mean?

The first 500 paid SentRules Primary licenses are eligible for the $19.99/year Founding rate.

Does the $19.99 price stay locked?

Yes. The plan is to keep that annual rate for a Founding 500 customer while that subscription remains continuously active. If it is cancelled and later restarted, the then-current price may apply.

What happens after the first 500?

New Primary customers move to the regular $29.99/year price. Existing active Founding 500 subscriptions stay at their Founding rate.

Can I buy Advanced now?

No. Advanced is not being sold until its feature set is ready. We would rather show “Coming Soon” than charge for unfinished software.

Is there a lifetime plan?

Not at launch. SentRules depends on Outlook and Microsoft platform changes, so an annual subscription is the cleaner promise for both the customer and the product.
